## Simple Summary
This OIP proposes to reinstate locking OVER tokens to earn protocol profit distribution while simultaneously stopping buyback and burn program.

## Motivation
Enahance token purpose and value by distributing healthy protocol profits to aligned token holders.

Current bear market pushing sell pressure on OVER token and buyback-burn program is providing liquidity to sellers.
Hence it makes much more sense to distribute weekly profits to aligned token holders.

Align token value more closely with protocol performance and usage.

Adapt to evolving market dynamics and user preferences.
The current environment and protocol state support a profit distribution mechanism.

The protocol's maturity and stable fee generation provide a consistent source for profit distribution.

Token Lock and profit distribution model protects loyal token holders and rewards them rather than providing exit liquidity to sellers.

Locking significant number of tokens will naturally decrease the selling pressure and soften up heavy market reaction.

Current protocol profits is above 30k per week range last couple of months and its feasible to support earnings distribution in bear market.

## Specification
The proposed changes are as follows:

Pause buyback and burn contract.

Design a new contract to reinstate token lock and earning distribution model.

its preffable to distribute more rewards to longer term lockers.

Ex. users locking 2 months or more are eligable to earn %100 of allocated rewards pro rata distribution for the week.

Users who lock their tokens for the first time start from %50 of allocated rewards pro rata distribution and reach to %100 in 2 months period.

if a user reach %100 and withdraw any amount , wallet earning allocation calculated depending on howmuch time wallet spend after reaching %100 and withdrawal amount.

if user reach %100 and withdraw all the amount, its earning allocation percentage reset to %50.

Weekly rewards distribution will be in USDC.
