## Simple Summary
Introduce a monthly batch distribution of unclaimed winnings in Overtime and Speed Markets to improve UX and reduce custody risk.

## Abstract

Currently, winnings not claimed within 90 days after market resolution on Overtime and Speed Markets are forfeited. This creates a poor experience for less active users and leaves the protocol responsible for holding funds during this period.

This proposal introduces a monthly batch distribution of all unclaimed winnings from markets resolved more than seven (7) days earlier. The seven-day buffer minimizes the risk of incorrect payouts due to potential odds or results corrections, while also ensuring that the majority of winnings—typically claimed within the first week—are excluded from the batch job.

## Motivation

** User Experience: Users who fail to claim winnings within 90 days currently lose them entirely, which causes frustration and reduces trust.

** Protocol Risk:The protocol temporarily holds unclaimed funds for up to 90 days, creating unnecessary custodial exposure.

** Efficiency: Past discussions ruled out autoclaim due to technical and cost challenges. A periodic batch process provides a simpler, lower-cost solution.

## Specification

Batch Distribution:
Once per calendar month, all unclaimed winnings from markets resolved more than seven (7) days earlier will be distributed directly to the winning wallets.

Execution Timing:
To optimize gas efficiency, the PDAO may execute the batch job at its discretion, provided it occurs at least once each calendar month.

Safeguards:

** Seven-day buffer ensures correction of potential result errors before payouts.

** Significantly reduces the pool of unclaimed winnings, since most claims occur within the first week.



## Summary

This proposal improves UX by ensuring that users no longer lose winnings due to inactivity, while reducing protocol-side custody risk. Monthly batch payouts balance efficiency, safety, and cost-effectiveness without introducing the complexity of continuous autoclaim mechanisms.
