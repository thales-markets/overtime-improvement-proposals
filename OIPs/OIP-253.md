## Simple Summary
This Meta Governance OIP proposes adjusting the monthly Overtime Council Stipends from 3,000 $OVER to 2,000 $OVER.

## Abstract
This Meta Governance OIP proposes to aligns governance costs with current protocol activity by updating the monthly Overtime Council stipend allocation to 2,000 $OVER.

## Motivation
During the first epoch of the extended 6-month term, governance operations consolidated, resulting in five passed proposals. This reflects the clear maturity of the protocol, which now benefits from streamlined Council oversight rather than high-frequency intervention.
Additionally, considering the appreciation in token value, optimizing the amount of $OVER distributed is the logical step to ensure treasury efficiency while maintaining fair compensation.

## Specification
This OIP entails the Overtime Treasury Council to update the monthly stipend distribution to Overtime Council from 3,000 $OVER to 2,000 $OVER per Council member.
